Misty guide: how Misty's own interface is laid out and how to do common things in it. Use it when the user asks about Misty itself. Quoted names are the controls' exact names, which also appear in any list of Misty controls sent with a screenshot. Positions assume the default layout (tabs on top); Settings › Layout switches the tabs between horizontal and vertical, so trust the screenshot and the control list over these positions.

There is no navigation rail and there are no Spaces or Agents buttons: the tab strip is the only bar. Spaces, Agents, Bookmarks and Settings are pages that open in tabs, reached from Cmd+T. A Space page lists its sections (Chat, Notes, Drawings, Files, Code) across its top, and Command with a number from 1 to 5 switches between them while it is focused.

Vertical tabs (a sidebar that can be dragged wider or narrower by its outer edge, and hidden with "Hide sidebar"):
- The sidebar's top holds back, forward and reload, the address field and, beside the site information button, "Pane layout". Favorites sit in a box above the tab list when there are any. "New tab" is the first row of the tab list.
- The bottom edge holds downloads and sync.
- The account panel floats in the window's bottom-left corner: the profile picture and name ("Profile"), then "Sync" and "Settings" (gear).

Horizontal tabs hold only tabs, with "New tab" (plus) after the last one. Everything else is in the browser toolbar. "Browser menu" lists Favorites and Bookmarks as panels that open to the side, and ends with "Settings", "Sync" and "Profile".

Tabs: each tab is a layout of one page, or several panes split side by side. Right-click a tab for "Pin tab", "Add to favorites", "New group" and moving it into a tab group.

Panes (the parts of a split tab):
- "Pane layout" (the layout icon beside the site information button) opens a menu with "Split right", "Split down" and "Close pane". "Close pane" closes the focused pane and is grayed out when the tab has only one pane.
- With two or more panes, pressing and dragging the empty space of a pane's toolbar picks the pane up: drag it to an edge to move it, or to the center to swap. Cmd+Option+M starts the same move from the keyboard (arrow keys choose, Enter confirms, Escape cancels), and then a press anywhere on a pane drags it.
- To close a pane, use "Close pane" in the "Pane layout" menu.
- Closing a pane leaves the tab and its other panes open.

Windows and Spaces:
- Every Misty window belongs to one Space, with that Space's own sign-ins and tabs; a Space has one window. Opening another Space opens its own window, as Chrome does with profiles.
- The window takes its Space's color, which tints its frame and tabs.
- "Spaces" (the Space name at the top of the window) lists the account's Spaces with a checkmark on this window's, then "New incognito window", "Space color" and "Close window". Cmd+Shift+N shows the same list of Spaces.
- An incognito window (Cmd+Option+N) belongs to its Space and keeps nothing: no history, sign-ins or site data after its Space's last incognito window closes. Agents never see it.

Browser:
- The toolbar holds "Back", "Forward", the address field ("Search or enter address"), the "Misty" button (the cloud) that opens the Misty panel beside the page, and "Browser menu" (more).
- "Browser menu" has "New tab", "Open Space", "New incognito window", "Reopen closed tab", "Downloads", "Find…", "Print…", "Translate page", "Reader view", "Share", "More tools", "Extensions", "Help" and "Settings".
- Cmd+T opens a new tab whose page is the search panel; Cmd+L, or clicking the address field, opens the same panel over the current page. Type an address, a search, a question or request for an agent, or the name of any page, Space, section or agent. ! narrows to one place (such as !files, !notes, !tabs or !yt) and @ and a name asks that agent. Enter opens the best guess; Command-Enter opens it beside the page. Cmd+Shift+A searches open tabs.
- Cmd+D bookmarks the page, Cmd+Y shows history.

Misty panel and Agents:
- Cmd+Shift+K toggles the agent panel beside the workspace, the quickest way to ask an agent about the current page. Misty is one of the person's agents.
- The Agents page lists conversations under Recents; each is a direct message with that agent in the person's own Space. "New task" starts one. The composer's "Message options" button holds "Plan", which researches and proposes a plan before changing anything (Shift+Tab turns it on or off while typing), and "Permissions": Ask pauses before every change for the person's OK, Auto continues on approvals they remembered and asks for the rest.

Settings:
- Open with "Settings" in the account panel or Cmd+,. Areas in the sidebar: General, Appearance, Layout, Shortcuts, Browser, Spaces, Agents, Sync, Devices, Privacy and About. Each area is one scrolling page; the search field at the top finds any setting.
- Layout switches the tabs between horizontal and vertical and keeps saved layouts. Agents › Companion sets up this cursor companion. Shortcuts lists and changes every keyboard shortcut.
