Misty guide: how Misty's own interface is laid out and how to do common things in it. Use it when the user asks about Misty itself. Quoted names are the controls' exact names, which also appear in any list of Misty controls sent with a screenshot. Positions assume the default layout (navigation on the left, tabs on top); Settings › Layout can move either to any edge, so trust the screenshot and the control list over these positions.

Navigation rail (the narrow icon column at the window edge):
- The Misty logo sits at the top.
- Destinations, top to bottom by default: "Browser" (globe), "Agents" (Misty's AI conversations and scheduled work), "Extensions" (puzzle piece) and "Spaces". The user can drag them into another order.
- "Spaces" is the window-like icon (panels). Clicking it opens a tray of the user's Spaces as avatars. The plus at the end of that tray is "Create Space". It makes a Space, not a window.
- When navigation auto-hide is on (Settings › Layout, or Cmd+Shift+B), the whole rail is hidden until the pointer reaches that window edge. This affects only the rail, nothing else.
- The fixed footer at the bottom holds "Search", "Sync", "Settings" (gear) and the profile picture ("Profile"), in that order.

Tab strip (along the top of the workspace):
- Each tab is a layout: one page, or several panes split side by side. A tab with splits has a small button that lists its panes.
- "New tab" (plus) follows the last tab.
- At the right end: "Create split right" and "Create split down" split the focused pane, "Close pane" closes it, then a thin divider, then "Manage virtual windows".
- Right-click a tab for "Pin tab", "Add to favorites", "New group" and moving it into a tab group. Favorites lead the strip in every virtual window.

Panes (the parts of a split tab):
- There are two ways to close a pane. Both are named "Close pane", so say which one you mean.
  1. In the tab strip: the always-visible "Close pane" button at the right end, after the split buttons. Its icon is a panel with an arrow, not an x. It closes the focused pane (click inside a pane to focus it) and is grayed out when the tab has only one pane.
  2. On the pane itself: a small tray at the pane's top-right corner that stays invisible until the pointer moves there. Hovering reveals "Drag pane" (drag to an edge to move the pane, or to the center to swap), "Arrange pane" and a small x, "Close pane", which closes that pane.
- If the user cannot see the small x, that is expected: tell them to hover over the pane's top-right corner, or use the tab strip's "Close pane" instead.
- Closing a pane leaves the tab and its other panes open.

Virtual windows:
- A virtual window is a separate set of tabs and layouts inside the same app window, for keeping projects apart. They are not Spaces.
- "Manage virtual windows" is the window icon with a small chevron at the right end of the tab strip. Its menu lists the virtual windows (click one to switch; right-click one and choose "Rename" to rename it), browser profiles, then "New", "Reopen", "Close" and "Clear history".
- To create one: open "Manage virtual windows" and choose "New", or press Cmd+N.
- Switch with Cmd+` and Cmd+Shift+`, or swipe horizontally with two fingers over the tab strip.

Browser:
- The toolbar holds "Back", "Forward", the address field ("Search or enter address"), the "Misty" button (the cloud) that opens the Misty panel beside the page, and "Browser menu" (more).
- "Browser menu" has "New tab", "New window", "New private tab", "Reopen closed tab", "Downloads", "Find…", "Print…", "Translate page", "Reader view", "Share", "More tools", "Extensions", "Help" and "Settings".
- Cmd+T or Cmd+K opens Search: type an address, a web search, or ! to search files, Spaces and bookmarks. Cmd+Shift+A searches open tabs.
- Cmd+D bookmarks the page, Cmd+Y shows history, Cmd+Shift+N opens a private tab.

Misty panel and Agents:
- Cmd+Shift+K toggles the Misty panel beside the workspace, the quickest way to ask Misty something about the current page.
- The Agents destination lists conversations under Recents. "New task" starts one. The composer's "Mode" menu beside the plus shows Act or Plan and switches between them; Shift+Tab does the same while typing.

Settings:
- Open with "Settings" in the rail footer or Cmd+,. Areas in the sidebar: General, Appearance, Layout, Shortcuts, Browser, Spaces, Agents, Sync, Devices, Privacy and About. Each area is one scrolling page; the search field at the top finds any setting.
- Layout moves the navigation and tabs and turns navigation auto-hide on or off (also Cmd+Shift+B). Agents › Models picks the AI models. Agents › Companion sets up this cursor companion. Shortcuts lists and changes every keyboard shortcut.
