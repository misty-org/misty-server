Misty guide: how Misty's own interface is laid out and how to do common things in it. Use it when the user asks about Misty itself. Quoted names are the controls' exact names, which also appear in any list of Misty controls sent with a screenshot. Tabs always sit in the tab sidebar, on the left or the right of the window (Settings › Appearance › "Tabs"), so trust the screenshot and the control list over these positions.

There is no navigation rail and no horizontal tab strip. Above the pages sits the Space bar: the Space's picture and name, which open its menu, then "Home", "Chat", "People" and the Memory icon, which opens the Space's memory tree. The Space bar can hide until the pointer rests on the window's top edge (Settings › Appearance). Agents, Bookmarks and Settings are pages that open in tabs, reached from Cmd+T. Focus mode hides the tab sidebar and the Space bar; each page then shows its own toolbar row.

The tab sidebar (it can be dragged wider or narrower by its outer edge, and hidden with "Hide sidebar"):
- The sidebar's top is the browser toolbar: back, forward and reload, the address field and, beside the site information button, "Pane layout". Favorites sit in a box above the tab list when there are any. "New tab" is the first row of the tab list.
- Downloads sit at the sidebar's bottom-right, with a progress ring while something downloads.
- The sidebar's closing row holds the profile picture and name ("Profile"), then "Sync" and "Settings" (gear).

Tabs: each tab is a layout of one page, or several panes split side by side. Right-click a tab for "Pin tab", "Add to favorites", "New group" and moving it into a tab group.

Panes (the parts of a split tab):
- "Pane layout" (the layout icon beside the site information button) opens a menu with "Split right", "Split down" and "Close pane". "Close pane" closes the focused pane and is grayed out when the tab has only one pane.
- With two or more panes, pressing and dragging the empty space of a pane's toolbar picks the pane up: drag it to an edge to move it, or to the center to swap. Cmd+Option+M starts the same move from the keyboard (arrow keys choose, Enter confirms, Escape cancels), and then a press anywhere on a pane drags it.
- Closing a pane leaves the tab and its other panes open.

Windows and Spaces:
- A window shows one Space at a time, with that Space's own sign-ins and tabs, and switches to another Space in place.
- The window takes its Space's color, a quiet wash over its frame and tab sidebar.
- The Space's name in the Space bar opens its menu: the account's Spaces with a checkmark on this window's, then "Open in new window", "New Space", "Rename", "Color", "Sites that open here", and "Leave Space" or "Delete Space". Cmd+Shift+N shows the same list of Spaces.
- An incognito window (Cmd+Option+N) belongs to its Space and keeps nothing: no history, sign-ins or site data after its Space's last incognito window closes. Agents never see it.

Browser:
- The toolbar holds "Back", "Forward", the address field ("Search or enter address"), "Agents", which opens the agent panel beside the page, and "Browser menu" (more).
- "Browser menu" has "New tab", "Open Space", "New incognito window", "Downloads", "Passwords", "Find…", "More tools" ("Translate page", "Reader view", "Picture in picture", "Print…", "Save page as…", "Copy link", "QR code…", "Open in default browser", "Clear browsing data…", "Developer tools", "Help") and "Settings".
- Cmd+T opens a new tab whose page is the search panel; Cmd+L, or clicking the address field, opens the same panel over the current page. Type an address, a search, a question or request for an agent, or the name of any page, Space, section or agent. ! narrows to one place (such as !files, !notes, !tabs or !yt) and @ and a name asks that agent. Enter opens the best guess; Command-Enter opens it beside the page. Cmd+Shift+A searches open tabs.
- Cmd+D bookmarks the page, Cmd+Y shows history.

Chat panel and Agents:
- Cmd+Shift+K opens or closes the chat panel beside the page, the quickest way to ask an agent about the current page. Misty is one of the person's agents.
- The Agents page lists conversations under Recents; a conversation lives on the device that started it. "New task" starts one. The composer's "Mode" menu holds Ask, Auto, Plan and Teach: Ask pauses before every change for the person's OK, Auto continues on approvals they remembered and asks for the rest, Plan researches and proposes a plan before changing anything (Shift+Tab turns it on or off while typing), and Teach turns the chat into a lesson that teaches one step at a time and never acts.
- An agent browses in its own small browser beside the chat, with its own tabs above its page; it never uses the person's tabs. Undocking shrinks that browser to a small picture at the top of the chat. "Take over" pauses the run and hands the person its pages; "Move to my tabs" moves one into the person's tabs.

Settings:
- Open with "Settings" in the sidebar's closing row or Cmd+,. The sidebar groups pages by feature. Settings: General, Appearance, Shortcuts, Voice, Privacy and Devices. Browser: Browsing, Search, Passwords and Extensions. Agents: Conversations, Cursor, Apps, Permissions and Memory. Each page is one scrolling page; the search field at the top finds any setting.
- General also holds links from other apps, sync, updates and Manage account, which opens the account, plan and billing on the Misty website. Appearance holds the theme, the night light, the tab sidebar's side and the browser's homepage and toolbar. Privacy holds website permissions, browsing data, approvals agents remember and sites agents don't act on. Cursor sets up this cursor companion. Shortcuts lists and changes every keyboard shortcut.
