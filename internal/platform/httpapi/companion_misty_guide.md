Misty guide: how Misty's own interface is laid out and how to do common things in it. Use it when the user asks about Misty itself. Quoted names are the controls' exact names, which also appear in any list of Misty controls sent with a screenshot. Positions assume the default layout (tabs on top); Settings › Layout switches the tabs between horizontal and vertical, so trust the screenshot and the control list over these positions.

There is no separate navigation rail: the tab strip and the browser toolbar carry the navigation.

Vertical tabs (a sidebar that can be dragged wider or narrower by its outer edge, and hidden with "Hide sidebar"):
- The sidebar's top holds back, forward and reload, the address field and, beside the site information button, "Pane layout". Favorites sit in a box above the tab list when there are any. "New tab" is the first row of the tab list.
- The bottom edge has "Spaces" and "Agents" buttons on the left. "Spaces" opens a menu of the user's Spaces with a checkmark on the open one, and a "Create Space" row that makes a Space, not a window. "Agents" opens Misty's AI conversations and scheduled work.
- The account panel floats in the window's bottom-left corner: the profile picture and name ("Profile"), then "Sync" and "Settings" (gear).

Horizontal tabs hold only tabs, with "New tab" (plus) after the last one. Everything else is in the browser toolbar: the "Misty" button (the cloud) opens Misty, which is the agent, and "Spaces" sits beside it. "Browser menu" lists Favorites and Bookmarks as panels that open to the side, and ends with "Settings", "Sync" and "Profile".

Tabs: each tab is a layout of one page, or several panes split side by side. Right-click a tab for "Pin tab", "Add to favorites", "New group" and moving it into a tab group.

Panes (the parts of a split tab):
- "Pane layout" (the layout icon beside the site information button) opens a menu with "Split right", "Split down" and "Close pane". "Close pane" closes the focused pane and is grayed out when the tab has only one pane.
- A pane also has a small tray at its top-right corner that stays invisible until the pointer moves there. Hovering reveals "Drag pane" (drag to an edge to move the pane, or to the center to swap) and a small x, "Close pane", which closes that pane.
- If the user cannot see the small x, that is expected: tell them to hover over the pane's top-right corner, or use the "Pane layout" menu instead.
- Closing a pane leaves the tab and its other panes open.

Virtual windows:
- A virtual window is a separate set of tabs and layouts inside the same app window, for keeping projects apart. They are not Spaces.
- "Virtual windows" is a submenu of the "Pane layout" menu. It lists browser profiles first, then the virtual windows (click one to switch; hover one for a pencil to edit its name and color or an x to close it; right-click one and choose "Rename" to rename it), then "New" and "Reopen", which opens a list of recently closed windows to bring back.
- Every virtual window has a color that tints its frame and tabs, so windows are easy to tell apart.
- To create one: open "Virtual windows" and choose "New", or press Cmd+N, then choose its color.
- Switch with Cmd+` and Cmd+Shift+`, or swipe horizontally with two fingers over the tab strip.

Browser:
- The toolbar holds "Back", "Forward", the address field ("Search or enter address"), the "Misty" button (the cloud) that opens the Misty panel beside the page, and "Browser menu" (more).
- "Browser menu" has "New tab", "New window", "New private tab", "Reopen closed tab", "Downloads", "Find…", "Print…", "Translate page", "Reader view", "Share", "More tools", "Extensions", "Help" and "Settings".
- Cmd+T or Cmd+K opens Search: type an address, a web search, or ! to search files, Spaces and bookmarks. Cmd+Shift+A searches open tabs.
- Cmd+D bookmarks the page, Cmd+Y shows history, Cmd+Shift+N opens a private tab.

Misty panel and Agents:
- Cmd+Shift+K toggles the Misty panel beside the workspace, the quickest way to ask Misty something about the current page.
- The Agents destination lists conversations under Recents. "New task" starts one. The composer's "Mode" menu beside the plus shows Act or Plan and switches between them; Shift+Tab does the same while typing.

Settings:
- Open with "Settings" in the account panel or Cmd+,. Areas in the sidebar: General, Appearance, Layout, Shortcuts, Browser, Spaces, Agents, Sync, Devices, Privacy and About. Each area is one scrolling page; the search field at the top finds any setting.
- Layout switches the tabs between horizontal and vertical and keeps saved layouts. Agents › Models picks the AI models. Agents › Companion sets up this cursor companion. Shortcuts lists and changes every keyboard shortcut.
